01 — What it is

The room is only the beginning.

Inside ORUNEVA, the place you sleep becomes part of the story. Iconic vehicles, carefully designed surroundings and seamless hospitality come together to create a stay people remember long after checkout.

A

Iconic vehicles

American school buses, Airstreams and other distinctive vehicles, restored and fitted out as rooms. The silhouette is the reason the stay is remembered.

B

Designed environments

A vehicle on its own is an object. Placed inside the right architecture, lit and served properly, it becomes a destination.

C

Seamless hospitality

Booking, pricing, arrival, housekeeping and guest support run on a professional property-management operation, not on improvisation.

02 — Berlin

A skyline you recognise, behind a building nobody is using.

Berlin still has what most European capitals have already spent: tall, raw, characterful industrial space, close enough to the centre to matter. That is the whole reason the first ORUNEVA is being looked for here, and not somewhere else.

Concept visuals. The tower on the horizon is the Fernsehturm — these frames show the kind of Berlin building we are looking for, not a site we have.

The Experience

Some places are functional. Others stay with you.

ORUNEVA is built around the second kind. We transform iconic vehicles into places to sleep, discover and remember — then build the environment and hospitality around them to make the experience complete.

The unit

The vehicle as a character.

An ORUNEVA unit is photographed and described the way a building is: silhouette, history, materials, the way light moves through it. Original fittings are kept where they carry meaning — the folding door, the destination blind, the ribbed flank — and everything else is rebuilt around comfort.

What the guest gets is a private room with a bed, storage, heating and a bathroom solution appropriate to the specific unit and site. What the guest remembers is the object they slept inside.

The environment

Around the unit.

A successful site builds a wider world around the vehicles. Not every location needs every element — the site determines the mix.

What matters

The things that decide whether a site can work

  • Character — a space with a presence of its own
  • Protection — covered, sheltered or partly roofed
  • Usable volume and internal height
  • Access for moving large vehicles in and out
  • Utilities potential
  • Guest circulation and privacy
  • Separate operational access
  • Room to create common spaces
  • Public transport and reachability
  • Local planning feasibility
What does not matter

The things people assume disqualify a property

  • The property does not need to look finished
  • It does not need to be a hall — courtyards and hybrid sites work
  • It does not need an existing hospitality use
  • It does not need to be central
  • It does not need to be pretty, only characterful
  • It does not need permits in place today

The concept may work precisely because the location has raw potential. We prefer protected and sheltered environments, because the vehicles should not be presented as permanently exposed.

Partnership models

The right structure follows the right location.

We do not arrive with a fixed deal. These are the structures we are open to exploring, and each is modelled against the specific site.

Lease

A defined commercial agreement for use of the space.

Hybrid

A fixed base plus variable participation.

Revenue share

Commercial participation aligned to agreed responsibilities and investment.

Joint development

A deeper partnership around site activation, works and hospitality operation.

Every model remains subject to commercial, legal, planning, tax and operational analysis. We do not promise returns, and we do not promise that a site can legally host the concept before site-specific assessment.

What you get from us

A use that suits difficult buildings.

ORUNEVA activates space that is hard to let conventionally: too raw, too tall, too irregular, or awaiting a longer-term plan. The units are self-contained, so the demands placed on the building are lighter than a fit-out that has to create rooms from scratch.

The result is a distinctive hospitality use, a reason for people to come to the address, and a visual identity the asset can be associated with.

Why it is structured this way

Three specialisms, one accountable brand.

Unusual accommodation fails in predictable ways: the object is charming and the operation is amateur, or the operation is competent and the object is generic. Splitting the roles is how the concept avoids both.

The location partner is responsible for the asset. The fleet partner is responsible for the units. The property manager is responsible for the stay. ORUNEVA is responsible for the experience the guest actually buys — and is the brand the guest books.

Cookies and tracking

      

        
Version 1.0 — last updated 12 September 2026.


        
This site sets no cookies

        
Not one. No analytics, no advertising pixels, no session cookies, no local storage, no fingerprinting, no consent-management platform. Nothing is written to your device and nothing needs your permission, which is why you are not being asked for any.


        
The one external request

        
The page loads its two typefaces from Google Fonts. That means your browser contacts a Google server, which receives your IP address in the process. No cookie is set by it, but the request itself is a transfer of data to a third party.

        
We are moving these font files onto our own server so that the site makes no third-party request at all. Until that is done, this is the only element on the page that reaches outside our own hosting.


        
Server logs

        
Our hosting provider records standard access logs. That is a server-side record, not a cookie, and it is covered in the privacy notice.


        
If that changes

        
If we later add analytics, embedded video, a booking engine or anything else that stores or reads information on your device, a consent banner will appear before it loads, non-essential technologies will be off until you accept, and you will be able to change your mind at any time. We will not activate any tracker before that mechanism exists.


        
Your browser

        
You can block or delete cookies for any site in your browser settings. On this site it makes no difference, because there are none.
